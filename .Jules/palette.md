## 2024-05-18 - Telegram Keyboard UX Optimization
**Learning:** Telegram custom keyboards (`ReplyKeyboardMarkup`) should always have `resize_keyboard=True` to prevent them from taking up unnecessary screen space and obscuring chat history, especially on mobile. Furthermore, presenting long lists of options (like countries, products, or operators) in a single column causes excessive scrolling and poor scannability; organizing them into multi-column grids (chunking) significantly improves the user experience.
**Action:** When implementing or modifying Telegram custom keyboards with multiple items, ensure lists are chunked into grids using helper functions (e.g., `chunk_list` in `utils.py`) and `resize_keyboard=True` is always passed. Ensure boolean or small option lists are also optimized for layout and sizing.
